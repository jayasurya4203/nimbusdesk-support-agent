NIMBUSDESK BILLING POLICIES
Last updated: 2026-07-14
Owner: Finance Operations
Applies to: Starter, Growth, Scale, Enterprise plans

1. BILLING CYCLES
=================
1.1 Workspaces are billed monthly or annually. The billing cycle starts on
the date of first paid subscription and renews on the same day each month
or year. If that day does not exist in a given month (for example the 31st),
the invoice is issued on the last day of that month.

1.2 Invoices are issued at 00:00 UTC on the renewal date and are also
available in Settings > Billing > Invoices. Finance Operations does not email
PDF invoices automatically; the workspace Billing Admin can enable
"Invoice notifications" in billing settings.

1.3 Payment is collected automatically using the payment method on file.
If the charge fails, NimbusDesk retries on days 3, 7 and 14 after the
invoice date. The workspace enters read-only mode on day 21 if the invoice
remains unpaid. Data is retained for 60 days in read-only mode before
offboarding begins.

2. SEATS AND PRORATION
======================
2.1 Plans are priced per active seat. A seat is consumed by any user who can
sign in to the workspace, including users who are invited but have not yet
accepted. Service accounts do not consume seats.

2.2 Adding a seat mid-cycle generates a prorated charge for the remaining
days in the current cycle, calculated to the day. Removing a seat does not
generate an automatic credit; the seat becomes available for reassignment
until the next renewal.

2.3 Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. Upgrades apply
immediately and are prorated. The plan's API rate limit changes when the
plan change takes effect.

3. TAXES AND CURRENCY
=====================
3.1 Prices are listed in USD. Workspaces with a billing address in the EU or
UK may be invoiced in EUR or GBP at the exchange rate published on the
invoice date. Currency is fixed at the time the subscription starts and can
only be changed once per 12 months.

3.2 Tax is calculated using the billing address and VAT/GST identifier on
file. A valid VAT ID removes VAT for EU B2B customers under the reverse-charge
mechanism. Changing a billing address does not retroactively change tax on
issued invoices.

4. PAYMENT METHODS
==================
4.1 Accepted methods: major credit and debit cards, ACH direct debit (US
workspaces only, annual plans), and wire transfer (annual contracts above
USD 10,000, Enterprise only).

4.2 Only a Billing Admin can update the payment method. Support cannot update
payment details, add cards, or process a payment on a customer's behalf.
Requests to change a payment method are account-sensitive and must be
handled through the self-serve billing portal or escalated to Finance
Operations if the portal fails.

5. REFUNDS AND DISPUTES
=======================
5.1 Refund eligibility, duplicate-charge investigations, and dispute
handling are governed by refund_and_dispute_policy.md. Finance Operations is
the only team that can approve a refund or issue a credit note. Support agents
must not promise a refund, credit, or waived fee.

5.2 If a customer reports a duplicate charge, an unexpected charge, or a
charge they believe is fraudulent, treat the case as account-sensitive and
escalate. Do not ask the customer to send full card numbers or CVV codes.

6. CANCELLATION
===============
6.1 A workspace Owner can cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. The
workspace remains active until the end of the paid period, then becomes
read-only for 30 days before data deletion is scheduled.

6.2 Cancellation does not automatically issue a refund for unused time.
Annual plans can request a prorated refund within 14 days of the annual
renewal, subject to review by Finance Operations.

7. BILLING CONTACTS
===================
7.1 Only the Billing Admin on file can request invoices, tax documents, or
billing changes. If the Billing Admin has left the company, the workspace
Owner must transfer the Billing Admin role in Settings > Members > Roles.

7.2 For invoice copies, direct the customer to Settings > Billing > Invoices.
Support cannot attach invoices that are older than 24 months without Finance
Operations approval.

8. SUPPORT BOUNDARIES
=====================
8.1 Support may explain how billing works, where to find invoices, and how
proration is calculated, using this policy as the only source of truth.

8.2 Support may not: issue refunds, apply credits, change a payment method,
edit an invoice, waive fees, extend a trial, or cancel a subscription on the
customer's behalf. These actions require Finance Operations or the customer's
own self-serve action.
