# Refund and Charge Dispute Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05
Owner: Finance Operations
Applies to: All paid plans

## Scope

This policy covers refund requests, duplicate charges, unrecognized charges, chargebacks, and billing disputes. It is the only authoritative source for refund eligibility. Support agents explain the policy but cannot approve refunds, issue credits, or waive fees.

## Refund eligibility

| Situation | Eligible | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First paid month, monthly plan | Yes, full refund | Requested within 14 days of the first charge and usage below 20% of plan limits |
| Annual plan, within 14 days of renewal | Yes, prorated | Reviewed by Finance Operations; unused full months only |
| Annual plan, after 14 days of renewal | No | Remaining term is non-refundable |
| Duplicate charge | Yes, full refund of the duplicate | Confirmed by Finance Operations after payment processor review |
| Failed or fraudulent charge | Case by case | Finance Operations investigates with the payment processor |
| Downgrade or removed seats | No automatic refund | Seat becomes available until next renewal |
| Add-on purchased by mistake | Case by case | Requested within 7 days and unused |

Refund decisions are not made in chat. The support agent collects the request and Finance Operations responds with a decision, normally within two business days.

## Duplicate charge investigations

A duplicate charge occurs when two settled transactions share the same invoice, amount, and currency within a 10-minute window, or when an invoice is charged twice by the processor.

Recommended steps for the support agent:

1. Confirm the invoice numbers and charge dates from the customer. Do not request full card numbers.
2. Check that the customer is not seeing a pending authorization and a settled charge. Pending authorizations usually clear within 3–5 business days and are not duplicate charges.
3. Verify whether the customer has two workspaces on the same payment method.
4. Escalate to Finance Operations with the workspace ID, invoice numbers, charge dates, amounts, currency, and the last four digits of the payment method.
5. Tell the customer the investigation target is two business days and that approved refunds post in 5–10 business days depending on the card issuer.

## Chargebacks

If a customer has already filed a chargeback with their bank:

- Do not promise a refund; the card network process takes precedence.
- Ask for the chargeback reference number if available.
- Escalate to Finance Operations immediately and mark the ticket as account-sensitive.
- Do not provide legal advice or speculate about outcomes.

## Unrecognized charges

Possible causes: another workspace owned by a colleague, an annual plan that renewed, a seat added by an admin, a tax adjustment, or a payment method reused from a trial. Support can help identify the workspace associated with a charge only after the customer verifies the billing email and the last four digits of the card. If identity cannot be verified, escalate.

## Refund timelines

- Finance Operations decision: 2 business days.
- Refund initiated to card: 5–10 business days after approval, depending on the issuer.
- ACH refunds: 3–5 business days.
- Credit notes: issued within 1 business day of approval.

## What support may say

Support may:

- Explain eligibility rules from the table above.
- Explain the difference between a pending authorization and a settled charge.
- Confirm that a request has been escalated and give the investigation target.

Support may not:

- Approve, deny, or estimate the outcome of a refund.
- Say "you will definitely get a refund".
- Issue account credits.
- Ask for full card numbers, CVV codes, or banking passwords.

## Data to include in the escalation

- Workspace ID and billing email
- Invoice number(s) and charge date(s) in UTC
- Amount and currency
- Payment method last four digits
- Whether a chargeback has been filed
- Customer's requested outcome
- Persona and frustration level, so Finance Operations can tailor the reply

## Legal and regulatory complaints

Requests that mention a lawyer, lawsuit, regulator, consumer protection agency, or formal legal notice must be escalated to the Legal team through Finance Operations. Support must not respond with legal interpretations. Acknowledge receipt, avoid admitting fault, and confirm that the appropriate team will follow up.
